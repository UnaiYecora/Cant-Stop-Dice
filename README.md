# Can't Stop Dice
### Automatic dice roller for the "Can't Stop" boardgame
I just made this simple dice roller for [Can't Stop](https://boardgamegeek.com/boardgame/41/cant-stop). It's just a little app that lets you roll four dice and it gives you the possible combinations, in case you don't want to do the math (or you don't have dice at hand).

[Open in your browser](http://unaiyecora.github.io/Cant-Stop-Dice/)
